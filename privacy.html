Privacy Policy

How Novanest Digital Solutions collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: January 2026

1. What We Collect

When you use our website or services, we may collect:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Business information (name, type, and requirements) when you request a website
  • Student information (subject, level, and topic) when you request tutoring
  • Payment reference codes when you submit a support contribution
  • Basic technical data such as browser type and IP address

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide the services you requested
  • Communicate about projects, tutoring sessions, or applications
  • Verify payments and support contributions
  • Improve our website and services

3. What We Do Not Do

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We do not send unsolicited marketing emails.

4. Data Storage

Your information is stored securely using Supabase, a professional database service with industry-standard security, including encryption and access controls. Only authorized Novanest personnel (the CEO) can access your data.

5. Your Rights

You may request at any time to:

  • View the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct or update your information
  • Delete your information from our systems

To make such a request, contact us at mickowenk@gmail.com.

6. Cookies and Session Data

Our website uses minimal browser storage to keep you logged in if you are a team member or administrator. We do not use third-party advertising cookies or trackers.

7. Children

For tutoring services, we may work with students who are minors. In such cases, we expect a parent or guardian to be aware of and consent to the tutoring arrangement. We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide tutoring.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The most recent version will always be available on this page.

9. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at mickowenk@gmail.com.
